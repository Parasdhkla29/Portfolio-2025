About Me

I turn raw footage into
stories that stop the scroll.

Hey, I'm Paras Dhakla — a video editor with a sharp eye for what makes Instagram content actually perform. I've spent the last few years working directly with creators across lifestyle, fashion, fitness, and travel niches.

My approach is simple: hook first, story always. Every reel I edit is built around the first 3 seconds — because that's where audiences are won or lost. From there, I craft a narrative that keeps viewers watching until the last frame.

I don't just cut clips — I obsess over pacing, sound design, color, and motion to give each creator a signature visual identity they can own.
